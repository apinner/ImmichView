ImmichView

Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026

ImmichView is a Roku channel for viewing photos and videos on an Immich server that you run or have access to. This policy explains what the channel does with your information. In short: ImmichView talks only to the Immich server you choose and collects nothing for itself.

What ImmichView stores

To connect to your server, ImmichView stores the following on your Roku device only, in the channel's local storage:

  • the Immich server address you enter,
  • the Immich API key you enter, and your Immich display name,
  • if you set a PIN for a user, a salted hash of it (never the PIN itself),
  • your ImmichView settings (for example slideshow speed and screensaver choices).

Removing a user in ImmichView's Settings deletes that user's server address, API key and PIN from the device. Removing the channel from your Roku deletes all of it.

User PINs keep people in the same household out of each other's photos on that Roku. They are a privacy convenience, not a password: the API key is still stored on the device.

Where your data goes

Your photos, videos, albums, people, search terms and other library data are requested directly from your Immich server and shown on your TV. They are not sent anywhere else.

  • ImmichView has no accounts, servers or cloud service of its own.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • The developer never receives your server address, API key, photos or any usage information.

Voice search uses the Roku keyboard's built-in voice input, which is provided by Roku. The text it produces is sent only to your Immich server as a search.

Roku and Immich

Roku collects some information about how devices and channels are used, as described in Roku's privacy policy. Your Immich server is operated by you or whoever runs it, and its own privacy terms apply to the data it holds.

Children

ImmichView is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
